# Business-question catalogue

**Proposed coverage:** 70 questions across seven journeys. These are user problems,
not 70 separate software modules. All questions remain subject to actual evidence,
applicable category rules and available tools. Question identifiers are stable for
traceability; A01–G08 extend the prior 56-question ideation, not a personal buying brief.

See the [product specification](product-spec.md) for functional requirements and
the [acceptance plan](acceptance-plan.json) for proposed verification.

## A — Feasibility, housing paths and timing

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| A01 | Which HDB, EC, condo or landed routes can this household consider? | Feasible/conditional routes and material buyer/category/ownership questions |
| A02 | Maximum five years old, but future launches are fine—what qualifies? | Explicit completed-age OR permitted uncompleted branch, with date basis and occupancy checks |
| A03 | Can we move by our deadline, including renovation and seller extension? | Practical/contractual milestones, buffers and timing failures |
| A04 | Buy resale, wait for a launch, apply for HDB, rent first or stay? | Consistent utility, timing, liquidity and transition comparison |
| A05 | Can we keep the current home, or must we sell before buying? | Separate ownership/use, loan, duties, CPF and timing determinations |
| A06 | What if TOP or possession is delayed? | Interim-housing, payment and household scenarios; no guaranteed completion claim |
| A07 | Will this still work as children grow, parents join or we retire? | User-selected life-stage scenarios and changing needs |
| A08 | Is entering the market sensible for our circumstances? | Need, resilience, transaction friction and alternatives; no unsupported timing prediction |
| A09 | How do joint ownership, tenure and intended use affect our choices? | Relevant facts, rights/restrictions and verification questions; no assumed workaround |
| A10 | Do senior, short-lease or other special housing routes suit us? | Specific eligibility, lease/use and later-life trade-offs; no generic HDB/private default |

## B — Discovery, alternatives and trade-offs

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | Where should we look if we only know our needs and budget? | Explicit geographic/category/transaction-route universe |
| B02 | What alternatives beat the project we already like? | Common-brief comparison beyond the first named development |
| B03 | Which requirements are making the search impossible? | Binding constraints, unknowns and the smallest proposed changes |
| B04 | What does another S$100k buy, or what do we retain by spending less? | Marginal function/access benefit, cost and actual liquidity implications |
| B05 | Is this an available home or only a starting-price/project possibility? | Unit/type identity, price kind, terms and availability state |
| B06 | How many distinct homes do these portal results represent? | Deduplication, suspected duplicates, exclusions and coverage |
| B07 | What changes if we value MRT, space, age or tenure differently? | Sensitivity/reranking without silently relaxing hard constraints |
| B08 | What HDB/resale control tests the cost of facilities or new-launch status? | Household utility and opportunity-cost comparison without prestige bias |
| B09 | Could improving our current home be better than moving? | Renovation/staying versus transaction and transition costs |
| B10 | Is there a better compromise between different household members' locations? | Person-specific commute burdens and negotiated trade-offs, not a misleading average |

## C — Daily routines, transport, schools and amenities

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | Is this close to MRT? | Gate-to-entrance route where obtainable, line, distance/time and measurement confidence |
| C02 | Can we comfortably reach several named destinations? | Door-to-destination trips by modes, days/times, transfers and frequency |
| C03 | Could we live here without a car, taxi or shuttle dependency? | Real daily routines, last-mile burden and fallback options |
| C04 | Is future MRT worth tolerating today's commute? | Current/future networks, waiting years, opening uncertainty and current inconvenience |
| C05 | Which schools or childcare are accessible and relevant? | School/service fit, official method/cohort where needed, routes and availability questions |
| C06 | Are groceries, food, clinics, parks and enrichment convenient? | Operating services, suitability/hours, access and dated sources |
| C07 | What noise, heat, construction, flight-path, industrial or flood issues matter? | Local/unit evidence and targeted viewing/technical checks |
| C08 | How could neighbourhood plans affect outlook, access and competition? | Confirmed/planned/speculative distinctions, location, timing and uncertainty |
| C09 | Does the route work in rain, with a stroller or for someone with limited mobility? | Shelter, crossings, stairs/lifts and actual accessibility limits |
| C10 | Do the building's everyday services support our routine? | Relevant lift, parking/EV, delivery, connectivity/work-from-home and service questions; operating proof where material |

## D — Documents, layouts, household function and building quality

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Find/read the e-brochure and explain what matters to us. | Accessible primary retrieval or supplied-file ingestion; page/version-linked facts and implications |
| D02 | Do brochure, price list, screenshot and listing agree? | Version/conflict reconciliation and affected outputs |
| D03 | How much is genuinely usable living space? | Advertised/strata/GFA/land/internal distinctions; supported measurements or explicit unknowns |
| D04 | Will a family of X fit comfortably? | Occupant roles, sharing/privacy, work/storage/service/access needs and confidence |
| D05 | Will beds, desks, wardrobes and circulation still work as children grow? | Source-backed clearances or a specific furniture/viewing test |
| D06 | Are there really three bedrooms and three bathrooms? | Approved/practical room use, access, study limits, full baths versus WC and HDB room labels |
| D07 | Which layout, block, stack, floor and facing should we choose? | Distinct layout/outlook/quoted-value/overall judgements and correct mapping |
| D08 | Could renovation make the cheaper home better? | Feasibility, approval/structural unknowns, cost/timing ranges and utility gains |
| D09 | What does building management and condition imply for long-term ownership? | Maintenance, reserves, levies/works, defects and evidence gaps rather than assumed project quality |
| D10 | What should we verify at handover or before resale completion? | Property-specific defects/approved-work/warranty questions and physical or specialist checks that affect the decision |

## E — Whole costs, funding choices and dated liquidity

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | What is the full cost of buying and living here? | Acquisition/fit-out/holding/transition/exit ledger with inclusions, exclusions and current-rule inputs |
| E02 | How much cash, CPF and loan funding is needed at each milestone? | Actual/estimated due dates, eligible sources and balances |
| E03 | Could we be short of money before proceeds, grants or approvals arrive? | Peak liquidity need and first dated shortfall |
| E04 | Should we use more cash, more CPF, or preserve particular reserves? | Feasible cash-first/CPF-first/reserve-preserving alternatives using the same household facts |
| E05 | What can we borrow versus what is comfortable to sustain? | Separate lender/HFE limits, household budget and reserves |
| E06 | What if rates rise, income stops or CPF inflows/family expenses change? | Household-level downside cash flow and runway |
| E07 | Can we carry two homes during a transition? | Linked sale/purchase dates, redemption, CPF refunds, duties and interim costs |
| E08 | Which loan, grant, CPF or repayment choice changes the decision? | Applicable verified conditions, uncertainty, receipt dates and practical trade-offs |
| E09 | What do lock-ins, refinancing, subsidies or early repayment cost? | Quote/contract-backed alternatives, fees/clawbacks and sensitivity; no assumed bank terms |
| E10 | How does this funding mix affect retirement liquidity and later options? | Cash/CPF restrictions, retained reserves and future housing scenarios under stated rules |

## F — Entry value, appreciation scenarios and exit quality

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| F01 | Is this asking/quoted price reasonable for this home? | Matched current evidence, differences, sample limits and conditional judgement |
| F02 | Is the advertised discount genuine current value? | Alternative/transaction comparison, incentives and actual terms |
| F03 | How has this unit type performed against comparable nearby homes? | Consistent windows/measurements, mix/floor/condition effects and gaps |
| F04 | What could support appreciation—and what could invalidate that thesis? | Demand/supply/access/lease/condition evidence and counter-explanations |
| F05 | What happens if prices decline, stay flat or rise at different rates? | Explicit scenarios/horizons and equity/cash outcomes; no invented probabilities |
| F06 | What exit price or PSF is needed to break even? | Clearly defined acquisition/holding/exit costs and debt/CPF treatment |
| F07 | Who might buy this unit later? | Layout/quantum demand, liquidity, competition, restrictions and uncertainty |
| F08 | Is renting or buying cheaper while retaining capital a better outcome? | Consistent utility, horizon, cash flow and opportunity-cost assumptions |
| F09 | Is a PSF difference really price movement or a measurement/unit-mix change? | Comparable denominators, layouts, age, sale type and sample explanations |
| F10 | Does appreciation before TOP compensate for construction, financing and waiting? | Common date basis, no assumed rental during construction, delay/downside and net outcomes |

## G — Action, challenge, updates and control of the case

| ID | User question | Useful decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| G01 | What should we view first and inspect there? | Feasible priorities and property-specific decision-changing checks |
| G02 | What opening offer, settlement conditions and maximum make sense? | Evidence-conditioned negotiation; budget cap separate from valuation ceiling |
| G03 | What is the strongest reason to reject our favourite? | Hard constraints, credible alternatives and downside challenge |
| G04 | Which missing information should we obtain next? | Prioritized requests by decision impact and effort |
| G05 | We changed household, budget, commute or deadline—what changes? | Dependency-linked rescreening/recalculation and superseded advice |
| G06 | Does a new quote, brochure, transaction or rule change anything material? | Source-version reconciliation and consistent outputs |
| G07 | Can we resume the case and understand previous exclusions? | Dated facts, assumptions, searches, recommendations and decisions retained |
| G08 | Watch these triggers and tell us when action is useful. | Explicit thresholds/dates/expiry, actual scheduling/channel status and authority |
| G09 | Can we challenge the ranking or see a different trade-off? | Explainable comparison, optional adjustable weights, uncertainty and hard-failure separation |
| G10 | What is stored, what can we export/correct/delete, and what may be shared? | Visible retention/provenance controls; personal/licensed material stays local unless sharing is requested |
