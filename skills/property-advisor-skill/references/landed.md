# Landed and strata landed homes

Landed analysis is address/parcel/title-led, not a condo stack template. Resolve
terrace (including corner/intermediate distinctions), semi-detached, detached,
strata landed and special/conservation/GCB context where applicable. Record
individual/strata title and tenure separately from physical form or listing label.

## Ownership and title

Use [SLA foreign ownership](https://www.sla.gov.sg/regulatory/foreign-ownership-of-property).
PR status alone does not eliminate foreign-person RPA approval requirements.
Sentosa Cove is not a blanket exemption. Strata landed within an approved
condominium development is treated differently from other strata landed; verify
the approved context rather than inferring from “cluster house”. RPA, taxes,
financing and any overlapping housing rules are separate determinations.

Research available property-specific title, boundary, easement/right-of-way,
tenure and approved-plan evidence. Public maps/listings are not a title search.
SLA INLIS is linked from URA property-data sources; paid/login access and extracts
are not assumed. Request only relevant permitted/sanitized records when needed.

## Daily suitability and condition

Assess land shape/frontage, access/parking/turning, road noise, neighbouring forms,
orientation, daylight/privacy, stair burden and accessible bedroom/bathroom options.
Multi-storey built-up space is not equivalent to usable single-level floor area.

Identify roof, waterproofing/damp, drainage, retaining/boundary walls, party walls,
services and renovation condition as evidence questions. A photograph or listing
cannot certify structural integrity, absence of leaks or flood safety. Explain
which inspection/report could change the decision. Strata landed also needs
common-property, management and recurring-fee analysis.

## Planning and financial analysis

Use current [URA handbooks](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/development-control/)
and [URA SPACE](https://www.ura.gov.sg/maps/) for applicable height/envelope,
setbacks, plot shape/width, access, conservation and detailed controls. Check
property-specific decisions and approved works if material. Existing built-up,
advertised area, GFA and future permissible development are distinct.

Do not use plot ratio × land area as a universal rebuildable-area formula. URA
handbooks are non-exhaustive; site-specific approval may differ. Do not promise
subdivision, extra storeys, A&A or rebuild rights without adequate evidence and
the necessary technical confirmation. Keep feasibility screening conditional.

Compare micro-location, form, title/tenure, land size/shape, building area/age/
condition and sale type. Keep land PSF and built-up PSF separate; neither is
directly comparable with condo strata PSF. Thin samples require disclosed ranges,
adjustment uncertainty or no value verdict, not manufactured quarterly trends.

Include repairs/A&A/rebuild and temporary-housing scenarios when relevant, with
assumed ranges versus actual contractor quotes clearly marked. Model upkeep,
insurance/taxes, financing, reserves and exit competition; do not assume “no condo
fees” means low maintenance or that every landed house has poor liquidity.
