# Executive condominiums

Start from [HDB EC sources](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/executive-condominiums).
Separate new developer purchase from open-market resale and any later policy
status. Record completion/TOP and applicable occupation/restriction dates from
evidence; do not infer unrestricted status from a marketing anniversary.

## Required checks

- Current household/citizenship/age/income/ownership/scheme conditions for the
  actual route, including disposal/wait-out or other conditions where applicable.
- Applicable grants, financing and own-stay obligations, separately from eligibility.
- Relevant MOP, resale buyer-pool and letting/use restrictions, including exceptions
  and the event/date from which a period runs.
- Availability and exact quote/terms, not only developer starting price or units sold.

Use [eligibility](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/executive-condominiums/eligibility),
[post-purchase conditions](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/executive-condominiums/conditions-after-buying-an-ec)
and substantive current sources. Several pages may expose little through text
fetching: mark extraction incomplete, inspect with available browser tools or
request a current official extract. Never substitute remembered “5/10-year” rules
for a case-specific check.

Use the private-non-landed/liveability references for plans, stacks, facilities
and maintenance. Compare matched EC transactions with relevant status, size,
floor, lease, age, sale route and condition. Non-EC alternatives may be useful
household choices, but do not silently mix them into an EC value baseline.

The configured URA EC launched/sold dataset contains volume counts, not a price
baseline. Research detailed URA transaction evidence or permitted extracts.
Apply the correct lending rules for route/status; EC purchases do not receive
an HDB housing loan merely because HDB publishes eligibility rules. Model fees,
holding/exit costs and restricted buyer pools separately from hoped-for appreciation.
