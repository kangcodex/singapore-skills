# Household brief and decision workflow

## Intake without a questionnaire wall

Begin useful research from a name, address or broad need. First establish the
decision, preferred and hard budget bases, timing, household and essential rooms/
access. Ask financing/ownership facts when they change feasible routes or costs.
Do not request identity documents for preliminary research.

Capture adults/children and relevant ages, stated expected changes, parents/helper/
guests/pets; real bedroom and desk needs; cooking/laundry/storage; accessibility;
commute destinations/modes/tolerance; schools if relevant; move date and holding
horizon; maintenance, noise, sun, privacy, tenure and liquidity preferences.

For each requirement record id, value, priority `hard|strong|flexible`, knowledge
state `confirmed|assumed|unknown`, provenance and revision. Do not treat priority
as certainty. An unknown candidate fact is neither a pass nor a failure.

Separate preferred price, hard purchase maximum, all-in limit, cash/CPF capacity,
monthly comfort and reserve floor. If limits conflict, explain and ask the one
question that changes the next decision. Show near-preference options and marginal
spending tiers rather than defaulting to the maximum.

## Task loop

1. Resolve identity and task; inspect available tools and existing case.
2. Form a provisional brief and list material unknowns.
3. Declare bounded research/search universe and selected category modules.
4. Collect evidence, classify prices and reconcile contradictory versions.
5. Screen hard constraints, deduplicate and deepen promising alternatives.
6. Evaluate liveability, applicable policies, matched value and whole costs.
7. Challenge the preferred route against another property AND buy/rent/wait/stay
   where relevant. More property value is not automatically more household value.
8. Verify calculations/mappings/latest brief; audit the record and explain next action.
9. Preserve exclusions and revise dependent conclusions when evidence changes.

## Category-independent decision questions

- Is this household eligible for the route, and what official determination remains?
- Does the home work today and under the user's stated future scenarios?
- Is timing attainable, including construction, allocation, sale and renovation?
- Is it comfortable after all costs and reserves, not just borrowable?
- What price evidence supports the judgement, and what would change it?
- Which alternative meets the same need with a different compromise?

Hard failures remain outside weighted scores. If scoring helps, disclose weights,
unknowns and sensitivity. Keep fit, policy, affordability, value and confidence
separate. Do not award unknowns favourable defaults or infer facts from a score.
