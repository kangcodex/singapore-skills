# Weather and flood evidence

Rainfall history is not a flood-exposure, drainage-condition or property-value
rating. The former HDB rule penalizing a premium for above-average rainfall is
retired. Unknown climate data must not become zero rainfall or proof of safety.

Use relevant local/property evidence, official PUB information and inspection
questions for flood concerns. PUB's overview does not classify each property;
absence from it is not evidence that a house is flood-free. Keep inland/coastal
exposure, actual events, building/drainage condition and mitigation distinct.
Do not infer local risk from the first station or an unordered nationwide series.
