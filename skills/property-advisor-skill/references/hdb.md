# HDB: new and resale homes

Read the evidence reference and current [HDB buying sources](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat).
These are mandatory research questions, not pre-verified eligibility outcomes.

## Classify route and household first

Distinguish new application/allocation routes (BTO/SBF/open booking as currently
offered) from resale. Record flat model/type, actual bedrooms, lease dates and
applicable Standard/Plus/Prime/legacy/special scheme, not merely town.
HDB “5-room” does not mean five bedrooms; verify the plan.

Check current household/citizenship/age/scheme conditions; relevant HFE status,
grants and loan eligibility; current/former residential ownership, disposal/wait-out
conditions; EIP/SPR quotas where applicable; MOP and future sale/letting rules.
Ask only needed household facts and never infer ethnicity. Quotas require current
applicable block/neighbourhood/household checks, not a static national table.

Use [HFE](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/flat-grant-and-loan-eligibility/application-for-an-hdb-flat-eligibility-hfe-letter)
and [framework](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/buying-a-flat/bto-sbf-and-open-booking-of-flats/finding-a-new-flat/standard-plus-and-prime-housing-framework)
pages and substantive linked conditions. Unknowns keep policy judgements conditional.
Do not apply one universal MOP, income ceiling, subsidy recovery or rental rule.
Short-lease/senior/special products need explicit eligibility and lease-use checks.

## Attainability and liveability

A project launch is an opportunity, not a confirmed unit allocation. Record
application/selection/booking status and timing separately. Compare waiting and
interim housing against resale without promising balloting success.

For resale, verify listing and unit details separately from transaction records.
Assess actual bedrooms, corridor privacy, lifts/access, service infrastructure,
noise, orientation, kitchen/laundry/storage and accessibility. Check permitted
renovation/structural limitations before assuming a layout can change. Identify
upgrading/planned works and any evidenced liabilities; do not assume they apply.

## Comparable evidence and finances

Use [HDB resale data](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_8b84c4ee58e3cfc0ece0d773c8ca6abc/view)
matched by block/nearby location, flat type/model, area, storey range, lease age and
time. Source uses labels such as `4 ROOM`/`EXECUTIVE`. Retain approximate-area
conventions and exclusions; storey ranges do not identify exact floor/unit.

Check applicable loan/CPF remaining-lease/age limits and valuation/purchase cash
gap treatment using current official rules. Do not invent a valuation, HFE amount,
grant or CPF entitlement. Separate estimated loan eligibility from comfort.
Include duties, transaction costs, S&CC, renovation and evidenced upgrading costs.

For keep/buy/let scenarios, research [private property acquisition](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/acquiring-private-property)
and [letting](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/managing-my-home/home-ownership/renting-out-a-flat-or-bedrooms)
restrictions. MOP completion alone does not resolve all ownership/letting rules.
