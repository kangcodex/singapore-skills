# Requirements-led, cross-category discovery

1. Translate needs into geography/commute corridors, categories/routes, price tiers,
   functional space, lease/tenure, move date and relevant policy questions.
2. Consider credible directions, including HDB new/resale, EC, flats/condos and
   landed unless excluded by the user. An unresolved eligibility fact produces a
   conditional route, not a confirmed fit or silent exclusion.
3. Search accessible inventory and official launch opportunities. Record sources,
   retrieval dates, actual query/filter boundary and important inaccessible areas.
4. Deduplicate reliable exact-home identifiers across portals. Retain suspected
   duplicates when identity is uncertain. Listing counts are not confirmed supply.
5. Apply hard constraints as pass/fail/unknown. Keep explicit rejection reasons;
   unknowns cannot satisfy essentials and preferences cannot offset hard failures.
6. Deepen a manageable shortlist with the best evidenced actual home/price or
   application opportunity, main compromise and fact most likely to change advice.
7. Compare view/research/quote/wait/revise-search actions, not just “buy”.

## What the user should see

Explain the universe, covered and missing inventory, near-preference options,
conditional fits, verified fits and labelled stretches. Avoid dozens of unexplained
projects. No-match is useful: propose the smallest useful change to budget,
location, timing or function, and obtain agreement before treating it as adopted.

HDB launch → application → allocation → selectable unit is not interchangeable
with a resale listing. Preserve route-specific attainability. Quotes need date,
identity, price kind/terms and availability evidence. No whole-market or real-time
completeness claim is warranted from partial public inventory.

Compare function, total spending/cash timing, monthly comfort, occupancy timing,
policy/use restrictions, lease, upkeep, future household needs and evidence quality.
Use category-specific value analyses underneath. Do not rank unlike homes by raw
PSF or imply HDB → condo → landed is a universal quality ladder.
