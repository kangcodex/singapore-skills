# Private flats and condominiums

Resolve identity, development status, developer, tenure/lease commencement,
completion/TOP/contractual possession, unit mix, actual unit and sale route.
Do not mix predecessor transactions with a redeveloped project. New/resale/subsale
comparisons must explain condition, timing, measurement and sale-type differences.

Research primary developer/contractual particulars, current quotations, URA
transaction evidence, listings, site/stack/floor plans and relevant planning.
Use available public sources plus permitted uploads. A starting price, price
matrix or press-reported discount is not an exact-unit quotation.

## Household and project assessment

Map type/block/stack/floor/unit using the correct plan version. Assess furniture
and desk usability, storage, laundry, service/yard space, balcony allocation,
accessibility and circulation. A 2+study is not automatically a 3BR.

Inspect real entrances and routes, roads, adjacent buildings/facilities/services,
orientation and floor-dependent clearance. Project nature views do not establish
this unit's view, quietness, thermal comfort or permanent clearance. Failed
panorama rendering means no inspection, not evidence of a good view.

Research project condition and management/maintenance where accessible: fees,
sinking fund, special levies, planned works and material disputes/defects as
evidenced. Missing management documents are unknown, not proof of healthy finances.

## Value and affordability

Use matched project/nearby transactions with relevant bedroom/layout, area,
floor, age, tenure/lease, condition and route. Explain exclusions/adjustments.
Keep total price, strata/internal/advertised area and PSF bases visible. Broad
district medians and regional transaction volumes do not prove exact-home value.

Budget duties, legal/loan costs, maintenance, tax, renovation/furnishing, reserves
and transition/exit as relevant. Use current buyer-specific tax/CPF/lender rules.
Separate a household's best layout from best location, best quoted value and
best overall fit. Unknown quotes or unit details limit the relevant recommendation.
