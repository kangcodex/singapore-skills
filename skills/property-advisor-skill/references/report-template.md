# Consumer decision report

Use concise prose and explained comparisons. JSON is the companion record, not
the consumer experience. Omit irrelevant sections rather than inventing content.

## Header and executive decision

Case/version, research date, household/budget assumptions, covered category/routes
and source boundary. State the next action immediately, the main reason, largest
compromise, conditional price/trigger and strongest alternative. “Wait/no match”
is a valid recommendation.

## Household and housing paths

Confirmed essentials versus preferences/assumptions/unknowns. Explain conflicts,
life-stage scenarios and budget tiers. Compare buy/rent/wait/stay or sale sequence
when relevant, with consistent timing and costs.

## Candidate comparison

| Home/opportunity | Fit / policy | Price kind/date | Attainability | Main compromise | Critical missing fact | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Group verified/conditional/stretch/rejected near-matches and explain inclusion.
For HDB new launches describe application/allocation rather than implying a unit
can be bought now. For exact homes include block/stack/floor/type or address/
parcel, area bases, quote terms and availability as applicable.

## Liveability and specific-home choice

Plan/source version and visual-inspection status; actual bedroom, access/privacy,
service/storage and future-household consequences. Separate best layout, location/
outlook, quoted value and overall fit. State mapping/view/measurement limitations.

## Price, affordability and policy

Report matched set rationale, dates/sample/adjustments, total/PSF/functional space
and confidence. No district-median exact-value shortcut. Present acquisition/
holding/transition/exit components and cash/CPF/loan timing; identify missing costs,
rule dates and assumptions. Show rate/income/reserve sensitivity and next spending
tier. Distinguish preliminary eligibility, approvals, comfortable affordability,
value and investment scenarios.

## Evidence, gaps and actions

Cited source register with claim scope/dates/confidence and conflicts; research
coverage and inaccessible sources. Prioritize viewing checks, quotations, mapping,
policy and specialist/physical evidence that could change the recommendation.
Maps/plans distinguish source information from annotations.

## Changes / watch specification when relevant

Old recommendation → new recommendation → reason; explicitly supersede advice
and keep prior record. Watchlist names, triggers, thresholds, dates/expiry and
actual schedule/channel status. Never imply notifications are active without one.
