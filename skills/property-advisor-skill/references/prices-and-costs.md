# Matched value, costs and investment

## Price and measurement discipline

Classify starting/indicative/asking/agent quote/unit developer quote/transaction/
valuation/hypothetical PSF scenario. Preserve dates, terms, identity and availability.
“Not actual prices” means area×PSF columns are scenarios. Discount magnitude alone
does not establish value; compare current alternatives and actual terms.

Match location/category, bedrooms/layout, area convention, lease/tenure, age,
floor, condition and sale route. Record sample/window, exclusions and adjustments.
Do not use a district median as sufficient exact-home proof. National volumes,
project medians and mixed unit types are context, not matched appreciation.

Keep SGD total versus PSF, monthly versus PSF/month, sqm versus sqft and land/
built-up/GFA/strata/internal areas separate. The helper converts only total→PSF
or monthly→PSF/month with known positive matching area; it refuses mixed land/
strata units. Comparable selection must still be justified independently.

Calculator amounts are decimal strings without grouping commas. Subtotals and
level monthly payments use Decimal and round half-even to cents; premiums retain
decimal precision. Trends round percentage changes to one decimal place. The
monthly illustration uses annual nominal rate / 12 and does not include fees,
insurance or lender-specific conventions. Statutory duty rounding must be applied
under the verified rule before supplying a duty component; the subtotal helper
does not calculate tax entitlement or rates.

Use completed calendar quarters for QoQ/YoY, not row offsets. Aggregate duplicates
using a justified method first. Label partial/missing periods and sample size;
do not fabricate eight bars. Thin samples can support no precise value verdict.

## Acquisition and sustainable affordability

Verify current applicable official duty, CPF and lending rules using buyer,
ownership, loan-product and transaction-date facts. Record rule/source/effective
dates, legal date basis, rates/bands, exceptions/remission status and inputs.
Property count and outstanding housing loans answer different questions.
MSR/TDSR/LTV applicability differs; an HDB loan is not a bank loan.

BSD uses the applicable higher consideration/market-value basis; do not assume
all promotional incentives reduce it. SSD schedules depend on acquisition dates
and legal transaction-date rules, not automatically completion. ABSD, remissions,
treaty treatment and property tax require their own current checks. Do not hardcode
entitlement from an old article or extrapolate an occupation period to tax exemption.

Separate price/deposits already credited, duties, legal/loan/agent costs where
applicable, renovation/furnishing, recurring fees/tax/insurance/repairs and reserves.
Add interim rent, double carrying, moving and exit costs as relevant. Do not count
deposits twice or call an incomplete subtotal all-in. Avoid double-counting loan
principal in cash-flow versus economic-cost comparisons; disclose methodology.

CPF is constrained funding, not unrestricted cash. Research lease/age/withdrawal
and retirement rules; sale refunds/accrued interest and redemption/costs/timing
affect usable proceeds. Do not invent valuation, grants or lender approval.

Show rate/income/reserve and holding-period scenarios; compare monthly comfort
with maximum borrowing. Explain what spending more buys and what it displaces.
Land repair/build costs without quotations remain explicit ranges/scenarios.

## Investment overlay and rent/buy/keep/sell

Distinguish evidenced and assumed rents, gross yield, net operating yield, financed
cash flow and appreciation. Include vacancy, fees, repairs, tax, finance and exit.
Use local/time-relevant demand, completions and competing supply; establish overlap
before summing unsold/pipeline series. Vacancy series count is not vacancy rate.
Missing demand/supply cannot become balanced. Test downside/base/upside assumptions
without guaranteed returns or justifying premiums merely because prices rose.

Compare housing paths on consistent horizons/assumptions, including sale-before-buy
versus buy-before-sale. Rental/letting feasibility needs applicable category rules.
Mortgages, cash flows and subtotal arithmetic are implemented; source/rule selection
and net-yield scenarios remain evidence-led analysis, not automatic recommendations.
